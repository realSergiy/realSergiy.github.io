## **The 1: The Nightly Balance**

Every night at precisely 11:14 PM, the Subconscious Registry begins its calculations. Maeven is a Level 3 Density Technician. Her job is not to interpret dreams, but to measure the exact mass of the words people choose not to speak before they close their eyes. For a human to achieve REM sleep, their mental buoyancy must hit a net value of zero.  
Maeven monitors a sector of 1,200 citizens. Her dashboard displays the mass of withheld phrases in real-time. The baseline metrics for unsaid words are standard:

1. **A withheld greeting:** 0.12 grams.  
2. **A correction left unsaid during a meeting:** 4.5 grams.  
3. **An avoided argument:** 12.8 grams.  
4. **A suppressed admission of a mistake:** 88.2 grams.

If a citizen accumulates more than 15.0 grams of unsaid words, they cannot float into the sleep state. They sink into insomnia.

## **Phase 2: The Anomalous Load of Subject 417**

At 11:32 PM, Maeven’s monitor triggers a yellow alert. Subject 417, a 17-year-old student, is experiencing a localized mass anomaly. His total mental weight has reached 342.5 grams.  
Maeven pulls up the itemized data ledger for Subject 417's past 14 hours to locate the ballast:

1. **14.1 grams:** An unasked question about a calculus theorem at 2:15 PM.  
2. **28.4 grams:** A joke he decided not to tell his peers at lunch.  
3. **300.0 grams:** A single, dense, unexpressed apology to his brother regarding a fractured bicycle frame.

The 300-gram mass is generating too much downward pressure on his consciousness. Subject 417 has been staring at his ceiling for 118 minutes. His heart rate is elevated at 84 beats per minute.

## **Phase 3: The Decompression Protocol**

Maeven cannot erase the 300-gram thought; clearing valid data violates Registry bylaws. Instead, she must apply a targeted decompression algorithm to break the mass into lighter sub-particles.  
She executes the following three steps:

1. **Step 1:** She converts 100.0 grams of the apology into a sensory recollection of the smell of wet pavement, distributing the weight across his olfactory cortex.  
2. **Step 2:** She converts 100.0 grams into a physical yawn, which vents carbon dioxide and reduces internal thoracic pressure.  
3. **Step 3:** She routes the remaining 100.0 grams into the "Next Day Action Item" queue, locking it into his conscious memory to manifest as a verbal prompt at exactly 7:45 AM the following morning.

## **Phase 4: Equilibrium**

At 11:41 PM, the telemetry data for Subject 417 updates on Maeven's screen:

* **Current mental weight:** 0.00 grams.  
* **Heart rate:** 61 beats per minute.  
* **Brainwave frequency:** 2.8 Hz (Delta wave entry verified).

Subject 417 drifts into sleep. Maeven closes the file, clears her display, and moves to the next citizen in the queue.
